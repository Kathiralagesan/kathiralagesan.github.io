Next-Generation Technologies

Analytical Method Development & Workflow Innovation

To tackle the immense micro- and macro-heterogeneity of glycoconjugates, we develop robust, high-throughput analytical methodologies that break traditional norms.

From protease multiplexing to EThcD fragmentation strategies — we continuously expand glycopeptide coverage and resolve complex isomeric structures.

Our Methodological Innovations

Sample Preparation

We apply and develop novel sample preparation strategies, including SP3, Mag-Resyn bead-based workflows, and multi-protease digestion strategies. Protease multiplexing with enzymes beyond trypsin — Lys-C, Glu-C, Asp-N — dramatically expands sequence coverage and exposes glycosylation sites hidden from conventional workflows.

MS Acquisition & Fragmentation

Advanced fragmentation strategies including stepped-HCD (sHCD) and Electron-Transfer/Higher-energy Collision Dissociation (EThcD) allow simultaneous backbone and glycan fragmentation. Coupled with FAIMS Pro ion mobility separation and DIA acquisition modes, we achieve unprecedented depth in glycoproteome characterization from clinical and biological samples.

Glycoconjugate Vaccine Development

PROT contributed to a landmark study developing a recombinant production platform for Group A Streptococcus glycoconjugate vaccines — cost-effective, scalable vaccines generated through in vivo glycosylation of carrier proteins in E. coli.

Our FAIMS-assisted nanoLC-MS/MS workflow delivered site-specific glycopeptide identification, glycan heterogeneity analysis, and glycoprotein integrity validation — including the detection of glycopeptides carrying up to 41 rhamnose residues. Full structural confirmation at this scale is a remarkable methodological achievement.
