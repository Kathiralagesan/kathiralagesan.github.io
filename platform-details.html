Proteomics Platform

Max Planck Unit for the Science of Pathogens

The Proteomics Platform provides access to state-of-the-art proteomics infrastructure and offers expert consultancy for both internal and external users.

At MPUSP, we use state-of-the-art mass spectrometry and chemical tools to monitor protein dynamics in microbes exposed to nutrient deprivation and unfavorable growth conditions. We are particularly interested in dynamic changes in protein levels, localization, interaction partners (RNA and protein), and post-translational modifications.

Our Multifaceted Approach

We integrate ribosomal protein profiling, transcriptomics, and proteomics to understand microbial behavior and its implications for pathogenesis, specifically exploring:

  • ■Host & bacterial proteome organization: Understanding how proteins are structured and arranged in both host and bacterial cells.
  • ■Bacterial Protein-RNA interaction: Studying the interactions between bacterial proteins and RNA molecules.
  • ■Infection-driven protein dynamics: Analyzing how protein expression and modification change during infection, including the complex cross-talk between different PTMs.
  • ■Stress-driven bacterial protein dynamics: Examining changes in bacterial proteins in response to environmental stresses.

Infrastructure

Mass Spectrometry

The laboratory houses two high-resolution hybrid mass spectrometers:

  • Orbitrap Exploris™ 480
  • Orbitrap Fusion™ Lumos Tribrid

Both feature a FAIMS PRO source for atmospheric ion mobility separations, allowing for improved analyte selectivity and expanded proteome coverage.

Liquid Chromatography

  • Ultimate 3000 RSLC (Dionex): Operated in nano or capLC configurations.
  • 1200 Infinity HPLC (Agilent): Used for pre-fractionation of complex protein digests.

Automated Sample Prep

Agilent Bravo Automated Liquid Handling Platform for high-throughput SP3, Mag-Resyn, and PTM enrichment protocols.
