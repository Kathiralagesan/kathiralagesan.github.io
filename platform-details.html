Analytical Capabilities & Workflows

We develop and deploy tailored LC-MS strategies across diverse biological models (spanning human clinical samples, eukaryotic model organisms such as Drosophila, and microbial systems) to address complex questions from discovery to targeted validation.

End-to-end analytical pipeline: From sample preparation, separation, and Orbitrap MS/MS acquisition to spectral identification, quantification, and downstream data analytics.

01 · Discovery & Quantitative Proteomics

Whole-Proteome & Secretome Profiling

High-depth DDA, DIA, and targeted PRM workflows across complex matrices, characterizing cellular proteomes, bacterial secretomes, extracellular vesicles (EVs), and low-abundance small proteins.

02 · PTMs & Chemical Proteomics

Modifications & Glycoproteomics

Targeted enrichment and gas-phase ion mobility (FAIMS/EThcD) for site-specific N- and O-glycoproteomics, intact glycomics, phosphoproteomics (including Arg-phosphorylation), and multi-PTM crosstalk.

03 · Interactomics & Complexes

Macromolecular Interactions & XL-MS

Affinity purification mass spectrometry (AP-MS) and cross-linking mass spectrometry (XL-MS) to resolve dynamic protein–RNA and protein–protein architectures and regulatory assemblies.

04 · Analytical Innovation & Automation

Workflow Engineering & Robotics

Next-generation micro-sample preparation (SP3), automated liquid-handling robotics, multi-protease multiplexing (GlycoPlex), and continuous performance tracking via the in-house Karuppar QC engine.

05 · Proteome Informatics & FAIR Data

Data Science & Open Dissemination

Rigorous statistical workflows, custom interactive visualization (VizGlyProt, Magnum), and standardized, reproducible pipelines powered by the modular SILAMBU ecosystem with open PRIDE deposition.

Infrastructure

The platform utilizes project-tailored proteomic sample processing workflows and quantification techniques, providing a comprehensive proteome-informatics infrastructure for rapid data processing and secure data storage.

Mass Spectrometry

The laboratory houses two high-resolution hybrid mass spectrometers:

  • Orbitrap Exploris™ 480
  • Orbitrap Fusion™ Lumos Tribrid with ETD

Both feature a FAIMS PRO source for atmospheric ion mobility separations, allowing for improved analyte selectivity and expanded proteome coverage.

Liquid Chromatography

  • Ultimate 3000 RSLC (Dionex): Operated in nano or capLC configurations for direct nLC-MS/MS interfacing.
  • 1200 Infinity HPLC (Agilent): Used for high-pH reverse-phase pre-fractionation of complex protein digests.

Automated Sample Prep

Agilent Bravo Automated Liquid Handling Platform for high-throughput SP3, Mag-Resyn, and PTM enrichment protocols.
