Research Focus & Physiology at MPUSP

At MPUSP, we integrate state-of-the-art mass spectrometry and chemical tools with ribosomal profiling and transcriptomics to monitor protein dynamics in cellular and host–pathogen systems exposed to environmental stress and infection:

  • Host & bacterial proteome organization: Understanding how proteins are structured, localized, and remodeled across host and microbial cells.
  • Protein–RNA interactions: Studying dynamic complexes between regulatory proteins and RNA molecules.
  • Infection-driven protein & PTM dynamics: Analyzing dynamic expression and PTM crosstalk (phosphorylation, glycosylation) during active infection and immune signaling.
  • Stress-driven proteome remodeling: Characterizing adaptive changes in translation, ribosome stability, and secretomes in response to nutrient limitation and cellular stress.

Infrastructure

The platform utilizes project-tailored proteomic sample processing workflows and quantification techniques, providing a comprehensive proteome-informatics infrastructure for rapid data processing and secure data storage.

Mass Spectrometry

The laboratory houses two high-resolution hybrid mass spectrometers:

  • Orbitrap Exploris™ 480
  • Orbitrap Fusion™ Lumos Tribrid with ETD

Both feature a FAIMS PRO source for atmospheric ion mobility separations, allowing for improved analyte selectivity and expanded proteome coverage.

Liquid Chromatography

  • Ultimate 3000 RSLC (Dionex): Operated in nano or capLC configurations for direct nLC-MS/MS interfacing.
  • 1200 Infinity HPLC (Agilent): Used for high-pH reverse-phase pre-fractionation of complex protein digests.

Automated Sample Prep

Agilent Bravo Automated Liquid Handling Platform for high-throughput SP3, Mag-Resyn, and PTM enrichment protocols.
