Software Tools & Pipelines

Development of robust bioinformatics pipelines for efficient data processing, peptide and protein identification, and quantification. We continuously evaluate multiple software strategies to optimize workflows — recognizing that no one-size-fits-all solution exists in proteomics data science.

Building interactive knowledge bases to facilitate comprehensive data exploration, visualization, and dissemination. Magnum Peptide Investigator

Protein sequence coverage maps are essential to proteomics — showing how peptides distribute across their parent protein. Magnum was developed as an independent, interactive tool for visualizing peptide sequence coverage across multiple samples, while providing a comprehensive heatmap overview of individual peptide intensities.

Example: Peptide mapping of Fetuin across 10 human tissue lysates with interactive protein detection side panel.
